Privacy Policy

How we collect, use and protect your information.

Last updated: November 2026

Information we collect

When you request a quote, send us a message, refer someone or submit a partner referral, we collect the information you provide. Depending on the coverage, this can include your name, phone number, email address, mailing address and ZIP code; vehicle details such as year, make, model, VIN and lender or leasing company; driver information such as number of drivers, age range and driving history; property details; business details such as industry, number of employees and annual revenue; your current insurance company and renewal timing; and any notes you choose to add.

If you enter your contact information in our quote form and agree to be contacted, we receive that contact information right away — even if you don't finish the form — so we can follow up and help you complete your quote.

How we use it

  • To prepare and send insurance quotes you request
  • To contact you about your request, including an unfinished quote, by phone, text or email as you've agreed
  • To share with insurance carriers as needed to obtain quotes for you
  • To understand which pages and ads bring people to our site, if analytics tools are turned on

Service providers

Form submissions are delivered to our agency email by a third-party form service (FormSubmit). Our website is hosted by GitHub Pages, and fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address. If we turn on website analytics, live chat or advertising measurement (such as Google Analytics, Tawk.to or the Meta pixel), those providers may collect information about your visit under their own privacy policies.

What we don't do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not ask for Social Security numbers or financial account numbers through this website.

Your choices

You can opt out of text messages at any time by replying STOP, and ask us to update or delete your information by contacting us.
