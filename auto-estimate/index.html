What affects your price

Driving record

Accidents, tickets and claims in the last few years usually have the biggest effect on price.

Credit-based insurance score

In South Carolina, many insurers use credit history when setting rates. Better credit often means a lower price.

Age and experience

Newer and younger drivers typically pay more until they build a driving history.

Coverage and deductibles

Full coverage costs more than the state minimum. A higher deductible can lower your premium.

Vehicles

The cars you drive, how many and how they're used all factor in.
