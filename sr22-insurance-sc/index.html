Common questions

What is an SR-22?

An SR-22 isn't a type of insurance. It's a certificate your insurance company files with the South Carolina DMV to prove you carry at least the state's minimum liability coverage.

Who needs an SR-22 in South Carolina?

The DMV usually requires one after events like a DUI, driving without insurance, a license suspension or too many points. The DMV or court will tell you if you need one.

How long do I need to keep it?

The DMV sets how long the SR-22 must stay on file. If your policy cancels or lapses during that time, the insurance company has to notify the DMV, so it's important to keep coverage active.

I don't own a car. Can I still get an SR-22?

Yes. A non-owner policy with an SR-22 filing can help you meet the requirement and get your license back even if you don't own a vehicle.

How fast can you file it?

Many carriers can file the SR-22 electronically soon after your policy starts. Call us and we'll explain the timing for your situation.
