Common questions

How can I lower my car insurance in South Carolina?

Comparing several carriers is the biggest step. Bundling with home or renters insurance, raising your deductibles, keeping a clean record and asking about discounts can also help.

Is the state minimum enough?

South Carolina's minimum is the least you can legally carry, and it may not cover a serious accident. We'll show you the price difference between minimum and higher limits so you can decide.

Will getting a quote affect my credit?

Insurance quotes don't affect your credit score the way a loan application can.

Do you work with drivers who have tickets or accidents?

Yes. We compare carriers that accept a range of driving records, including drivers who need an SR-22.
