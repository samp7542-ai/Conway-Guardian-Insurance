Common questions

Does my homeowners policy cover a house I rent out?

Usually not. A homeowners policy is designed for a home you live in. A rental property generally needs a landlord (dwelling fire) policy.

Does landlord insurance cover my tenant's belongings?

No. Tenants need their own renters insurance for their belongings. Many landlords require it in the lease.

What about loss of rent?

Many landlord policies can include loss-of-rent coverage, which helps replace rental income if a covered loss makes the home unlivable.

Can you insure a vacant property?

Vacant properties often need special coverage. Tell us about the property and we'll look for options.
